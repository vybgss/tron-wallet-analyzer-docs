Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version

Tron Wallet Analyzer collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK and no server of ours. Your wallets, contacts, watchlist and check history stay on your device. To read public blockchain data, the app asks TronGrid, Tronscan and GitHub directly from your device; nothing passes through us.

On your device

1. What the app keeps, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • addresses you mark as your own wallets or save as contacts, with the names you give them;
  • your watchlist and the monitoring events it produced;
  • the history of your checks;
  • cached answers from TronGrid and Tronscan, kept for a few minutes to save your API quota;
  • reference data: the public OFAC list and an index of Tether's public freeze events;
  • your settings, such as the theme, the language and the monitoring interval.

None of it is synced to iCloud or any other service, and none of it is sent to us. Settings → Erase All Local Data deletes your wallets, contacts, watchlist, history and cached answers; deleting the app removes everything.

2. API keys

If you add free TronGrid or Tronscan API keys, they are saved in the Keychain of this device only, are not synced to your other devices, and are sent only to the service that issued them, with that service's requests. They never appear in reports, exports or logs.

Network

3. Who the app talks to

The app reads public data from three services, directly from your device:

  • TronGrid (api.trongrid.io) — accounts, balances, USDT transfers, Tether's freeze state and freeze events, energy and network prices;
  • Tronscan (apilist.tronscanapi.com) — account details, public name tags and Tether freeze records;
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) — the public list of OFAC-sanctioned Tron addresses.

A request contains the public Tron addresses being looked up and, if you added one, your API key for that service. Like any internet request, it also reveals your IP address to the service. Each service handles its requests under its own privacy policy. The app sends nothing else: not your other wallets, not your contacts, not your history.

Links in the app, such as a transaction on Tronscan, open in your browser only when you choose to follow them.

4. Monitoring and notifications

The watchlist is checked on your device: when the app opens, through Background App Refresh on iPhone and iPad, and on the interval you choose on Mac. Notifications are local notifications created on the device; there is no push server, and the app asks for permission before sending any.

Device features

5. Camera

On iPhone and iPad, the camera is used only when you choose to scan an address QR code. The picture is read on the device and is neither saved nor sent anywhere.

6. Clipboard

On iPhone and iPad, the app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste. On Mac, the optional Clipboard Guard (off by default) reads copied text while it is on, to compare a copied Tron address with your wallets and contacts and warn about a lookalike. The comparison happens on the device and nothing is stored.

7. Reports and exports

PDF reports and CSV files are created on your device and go only where you send them with the system share sheet or save panel.

Purchases

8. Pro

Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details and no information that identifies you. The app checks your Pro status on the device with Apple's StoreKit. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account.

Everything else

9. No analytics or tracking

There is no analytics framework, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party code in the app. We do not know who you are, whether you use the app, or which addresses you check. If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass them to us without anything that identifies you; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

10. Read-only by design

The app never asks for private keys, seed phrases or signatures, and it cannot send, receive or move funds. It is not a wallet.

11. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
