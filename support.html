Getting started

What is Tron Wallet Analyzer?

An app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that checks Tron addresses which send or receive USDT (TRC-20). Before you pay someone, or after someone pays you, it shows the public facts about an address: whether Tether has frozen its USDT, whether it is on the OFAC sanctions list, and whether it has exchanged USDT with addresses that are. It reads only public data, needs no account, and never asks for keys.

How do I check an address?

Open the Check tab, paste the address with Paste or type it, and tap Check. On iPhone and iPad, Scan QR reads it from a QR code. The result shows the color, every finding with its reason and transactions, the account (type, activation date, TRX and USDT balances, transaction count) and what sending USDT to that address costs. From there you can open its graph, copy it, add it to your contacts, mark it as your own or watch it.

What do the colors mean?

Each color comes only from verifiable facts, and each fact is listed with its link:

  • High Tether has frozen the USDT on this address, Tether has destroyed its frozen balance, or the address is on the OFAC sanctions list.
  • Caution The address was frozen by Tether earlier and later unfrozen, it has direct USDT transfers with a frozen or sanctioned address, or it received zero or dust transfers from a lookalike of one of its counterparties.
  • No findings None of the above in the checked scope. It is not a guarantee: it means only that the checked data contains no such facts.

A flagged address two steps away is shown as information and does not change the color.

Tron Wallet Analyzer is an information tool. Colors summarize public on-chain facts and public lists within the checked scope; they are not a legal, compliance or investment opinion about a person or funds. Verify every finding through its link before acting.

Where do the facts come from?

  • Tether's USDT contract on TRON, through TronGrid: the live freeze state of the address and the history of Tether's freeze, unfreeze and destroy events.
  • The OFAC sanctions list: the Tron addresses on it, from the public list maintained on GitHub (0xB10C/ofac-sanctioned-digital-currency-addresses).
  • TronGrid and Tronscan: accounts, balances, USDT transfers and public name tags.

The OFAC list and an index of Tether's freeze events are downloaded to your device and kept up to date; their state is in Settings → Reference Data.

What are the limits of a check?

Every result lists what it could not cover, for example: no API key (slower requests, no name tag), only the newest transfers of a very busy address analyzed, the local index of Tether freezes still downloading on first launch, or a service that did not answer. Run the check again later to fill the gaps.

Features

How does the transfer graph work?

In the Graph tab, enter an address or tap Graph on a check. Nodes are addresses and edges are USDT transfers with totals and dates; nodes are green, yellow or red by the same facts as the check. Tap a node for its reasons and transfers, or to expand it one more step. Filter by minimum amount and period, zoom and pan with the controls in the corner.

How do I audit my wallets?

In Wallets → My Wallets, add your addresses (only addresses, no keys) and tap Analyze Incoming and Outgoing. You see who sent you USDT and where you sent it, the totals and dates, and an audit of every counterparty with its findings. The “…” menu of a wallet has Check, Graph, Energy & Fees, Rename, Watch and Not My Wallet.

How does it catch address poisoning?

Address poisoning sends you a zero or tiny transfer from an address that starts and ends like one you use, so it shows up in your history and gets copied by mistake. Save the addresses you have verified in Wallets → Contacts. The app then warns about lookalikes — same first and last characters, different address — and about zero or dust transfers from them, and shows full addresses with every differing character highlighted. Compare Two Addresses checks any pair. On Mac, the optional Clipboard Guard in the Monitor tab compares every Tron address you copy with your wallets and contacts.

How does monitoring work?

Add addresses to the watchlist in the Monitor tab, or tap Watch on a check. The watchlist is checked when the app opens; on iPhone and iPad also through Background App Refresh, at times iOS chooses; on Mac every 15, 30, 60 or 180 minutes while the app is running, also with the window closed. You get a notification for new incoming USDT, when a watched address is frozen by Tether, and when a counterparty becomes frozen or sanctioned. If notifications don't arrive, check that they are allowed for the app and, on iPhone and iPad, that Background App Refresh is on in the system Settings.

What is Energy & Fees?

A USDT transfer on TRON uses energy and bandwidth, paid by the sender. Without staked energy, TRX is burned instead. Open Energy & Fees from a wallet's “…” menu to see its energy and bandwidth, the TRX one USDT transfer from it burns now, and how much TRX to stake for a number of transfers per day, with the break-even point. Each check also shows what sending USDT to that address costs: about twice the energy when the address holds no USDT. Prices come live from the network. It is a calculation, not financial advice.

Reports and exports

A check can be saved as a PDF Report with the date, the sources and the transaction links. Transfers, graphs and counterparty audits export as CSV. Files are created on your device and go where you send them.

API keys

Do I need API keys?

No, but they help. Without keys the app works in a limited mode: requests to TronGrid are slowed to about one per second, and Tronscan's name tag and transaction count are not shown. Both keys are free:

  • TronGrid: sign in at trongrid.io and create an API key.
  • Tronscan: sign in at tronscan.org and create an API key in your account's API keys section.

Paste them in Settings → API Keys and tap Test Key. Keys are stored only in this device's Keychain and sent only to the service that issued them.

Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: 3 address checks a day, checks and graphs one step deep, wallets and their audit, contacts, lookalike warnings, address compare and Energy & Fees.

Pro adds unlimited checks, checks and graphs two steps deep, watchlist monitoring with notifications, and PDF and CSV export. It is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Tap Get Pro on the Check tab or See Plans in Settings.

Restore, cancel or change a plan

  • New device or reinstall: tap Restore Purchases on the plans screen or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel or change a subscription: in Settings → Manage Subscription, or in your Apple Account's subscriptions. Pro stays until the end of the paid period.
  • Lifetime with a running subscription: Apple does not cancel the subscription for you. Cancel it in your Apple Account if you no longer need it.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy and safety

Does the app need my private key or seed phrase?

Never. Tron Wallet Analyzer is read-only: it works with public addresses only, never asks for private keys, seed phrases or signatures, and cannot send, receive or move funds. It is not a wallet. If anyone asks you for your seed phrase in the app's name, it is not us.

Where is my data, and how do I erase it?

Your wallets, contacts, watchlist and check history stay on your device and are not synced. Settings → Erase All Local Data deletes them together with the cached answers; API keys stay in the Keychain until you clear them. See the Privacy Policy.

Settings

Language and themes

The app speaks English, German, French, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese. It follows your device language, falling back to English, and you can change it in Settings → Appearance or in the system's per-app language setting. Five themes are there too: Midnight (the default), Ocean, Serene, Cosmic and the light Pure.

System requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, iPad with iPadOS 26 or later, or a Mac with macOS 26 or later. An internet connection is needed to check addresses.
